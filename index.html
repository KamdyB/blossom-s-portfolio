02 — A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Care about the details.

Code matters. So does the thinking around it.

I like the moment a loose question becomes something you can test, read, or put in someone’s hands.

I study Computer Science at Babcock University, and my interests keep crossing between software engineering, data science, AI, and sports analytics. Sometimes that means making a small product feel less awkward to use; sometimes it means checking what a dataset can actually support before telling a story with it. I’m interested in the work behind the result as much as the result itself.

Alongside building, I write about technology and contribute to campus tech communities. I’m still learning, and I prefer showing the state of a project honestly over dressing it up as something finished. The point is to keep getting better at asking useful questions and making the answers clearer.
